Symbiocen

Regenerative urban design, made explorable.

Symbiocen is an interactive urban digital twin for early-stage building design. It brings whole-life carbon, operational energy, daylight, structural behavior, ecology, and climate intelligence into one shared design surface.

Designed by Aarti S Ravikumar for the ACE Program at PCSS II.

What is Symbiocen?

Symbiocen is a regenerative architecture and urban-design prototype that lets designers alter massing, orientation, envelope, structure, landscape, and renewable-energy choices while inspecting their coupled consequences. The 3D digital twin supports comparative exploration rather than professional certification.

Design performance in one workflow

  • Whole-life carbon: embodied carbon, biogenic storage, material circularity, and an EC3-inspired inventory.
  • Operational energy: energy-use intensity, photovoltaic yield, and net-zero readiness.
  • Daylight and comfort: spatial daylight autonomy, annual sunlight exposure, glare risk, and illuminance.
  • Structure and ecology: wind deflection, drift checks, green cover, stormwater retention, and biodiversity signals.

Multi-agent regenerative design council

Architect, engineer, constructor, and ecologist perspectives interpret the current model together. Their recommendations help make trade-offs visible across mass timber, prefabrication, climate response, circular materials, habitat, and urban resilience.

Method and responsible use

Symbiocen uses transparent deterministic surrogate equations for rapid comparative feedback. It is educational decision support, not a substitute for validated energy modeling, finite-element analysis, certified environmental product declarations, code review, or licensed professional judgment.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Symbiocen for?

It is designed for students, designers, educators, architects, engineers, constructors, and urban ecologists exploring regenerative building decisions.

What can it analyze?

It offers immediate comparative signals for carbon, energy, daylight, structural behavior, ecological performance, materials, and climate-responsive strategies.

Is it a certified building-analysis tool?

No. Symbiocen is a research and educational prototype. Project decisions should be verified with appropriate validated tools and qualified professionals.
